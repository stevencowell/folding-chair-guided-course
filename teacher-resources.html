Confirmed drawing details

Keep critical dimensions and assembly details under source control

The approved drawing records 384 mm overall width, 780 mm overall height and 405 mm seat height. Pivot assembly uses M6 × 50 countersunk bolts, washers and locknuts. Trenches are 6 mm deep, and final component lengths are custom-fitted after trenching.

Machine setup, tolerances, practical permissions, finish selection, repair decisions and local SOP/SWMS requirements remain teacher controlled.

Evidence boundary

Browser records are working copies

The webpage does not transmit or verify marks. A saved PDF is student evidence, not an automatically authenticated test record. Submission remains through the route directed by the teacher.
